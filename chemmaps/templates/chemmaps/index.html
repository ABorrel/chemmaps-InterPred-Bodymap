ChemMaps

ChemMaps is a way to browse large chemical libraries on a map. Pick one of the four libraries below.

DrugMap

DrugMap shows marketed and experimental drugs from DrugBank 5.1.10, released 4 January 2023.

Open DrugMap

DSSToxMap

DSSToxMap shows environmental chemicals from the EPA DSSTox library, released February 2022.

Open DSSToxMap

Tox21Map

Tox21Map shows the Tox21 screening library, released 29 October 2022.

Open Tox21Map

PFASMap

PFASMap shows PFAS structures, released 29 October 2022.

Open PFASMap
